Section 01

About Bengal Tree Foundation

Bengal Tree Foundation is a government-registered environmental NGO headquartered in Barasat, Kolkata, West Bengal. Established in 2024, the Foundation focuses exclusively on tree plantation, urban green-cover restoration, and bird conservation across Bengal — engaging communities, volunteers, and corporate CSR partners through science-backed plantation programmes.

Mission Statement

To plant and nurture trees across West Bengal through community participation, scientific monitoring, and corporate CSR partnerships — ensuring every sapling becomes a thriving, documented, and permanently protected tree that benefits both nature and people.

Section 02

Project Overview

Systematic plantation of tall, certified, nursery-raised native and fruit-bearing saplings (7–8 ft minimum) across Kolkata's urban and peri-urban landscape. Every tree is planted with full site preparation, soil enrichment, guard protection, and 12-month professional maintenance — with complete geo-tagged documentation and quarterly survival reporting.

Section 03

Implementation Process

1
Site Assessment & Preparation
  • GPS mapping of plantation sites with local bodies
  • Soil testing & suitability assessment
  • KMC / panchayat permission liaison
  • Pit excavation (2×2×2 ft) with organic compost + FYM mix
2
Monthly Plantation Drive
  • Certified 7–8 ft nursery saplings procured
  • Transport & on-site care
  • Plantation with wooden stake support
  • Organic manure at root zone
  • Tree guard installation with number tagging
3
12-Month Maintenance
  • Daily / alternate watering schedule
  • Monthly weeding & soil loosening
  • Bio-pesticide & organic fertiliser
  • 30-day mortality replacement
  • Geo-tagged photo documentation
  • Quarterly survival audit reports

CSR Compliance & 80G Documents

Schedule VII — Statutory Basis

Tree plantation and environmental sustainability programmes are explicitly covered under Schedule VII of the Companies Act, 2013. Applicable item: (iv) "ensuring environmental sustainability, ecological balance, protection of flora and fauna, animal welfare, agroforestry, conservation of natural resources and maintaining quality of soil, air and water." Kisna's contribution is fully compliant Schedule VII CSR expenditure.
